FAQ

Before you choose a technology partner.

Straight answers to the questions we hear most. Something else on your mind? Ask us directly.

What does Norvexa actually do?+

We help companies put AI and modern software to work: AI agents, a company-wide AI assistant, custom AI apps, plus full-stack web, mobile, SaaS, e-commerce and cloud engineering.

Where should we start with AI?+

With the AI audit. In two to four weeks we map your processes and systems, score opportunities and hand over a prioritised roadmap with a business case.

Will AI replace our team?+

No. Our agents take on repetitive work so your people can focus on judgement, customers and growth. Humans approve every decision that matters.

Is our data safe?+

Yes. Solutions run on your own cloud or servers, use role-based access, log every action, and use enterprise AI endpoints that do not train on your data.

Which AI models do you use?+

We are model-agnostic and use GPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Mistral and others, choosing the providers your company approves for each job.

Do we need to replace our existing software?+

Rarely. We integrate with the CRM, ERP, email, chat and document tools you already use.

How long does a typical project take?+

An AI audit takes 2 to 4 weeks. A first AI agent or a corporate website typically takes 7 to 10 weeks. Larger platforms are delivered in phases.

Who owns the code and IP?+

You do: source code, prompts, configurations and data. No lock-in.

Do you work with clients outside India?+

Yes. We serve clients in the USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia, Germany and across Europe, with time-zone-aligned communication.
