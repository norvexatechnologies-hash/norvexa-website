How Much Does Custom Software Development Cost?

What drives the cost of custom software: scope, complexity, integrations, team model, and how to budget sensibly for build and long-term support.

Norvexa Editorial Team·8 October 2026·7 min read

Why there is no single price

Asking what custom software costs is like asking what a building costs. The answer depends on size, complexity, materials and location. What you can do is understand the main cost drivers and use them to set a realistic budget range.

The main cost drivers

These factors account for most of the variation between estimates:

  • Scope: the number of user roles, screens, workflows and reports
  • Complexity: business rules, calculations, real-time features and AI
  • Integrations: connections to ERP, CRM, payment gateways and identity providers
  • Platforms: web only, or web plus iOS and Android
  • Non-functional requirements: security, compliance, performance and availability
  • Team model and location

Fixed price or dedicated team?

For a clear, stable scope, a fixed price gives budget certainty. For products that will evolve with user feedback, a dedicated team with a monthly cost is usually more efficient, because you are not renegotiating every change.

Do not forget the cost of ownership

Launch is the beginning. Budget for hosting, monitoring, security updates, third-party licences and ongoing improvements. A common rule of thumb is to plan 15 to 25 percent of the initial build cost per year for maintenance and enhancements.

How to reduce cost without cutting corners

Start with a focused first release that solves the most valuable problem, reuse proven components, integrate rather than rebuild where possible, and invest in a discovery phase so the estimate is based on facts rather than assumptions.
