FAQ

About these estimates

How accurate is this estimate?+

It gives a realistic planning range based on typical projects of each type. Your exact price depends on detailed requirements, which we confirm in a short discovery call before quoting.

Why is there a range rather than a single price?+

Two apps with the same feature list can differ a lot in complexity, integrations and design. The range covers a simple and a more complex version of what you selected.

Can I start smaller and add features later?+

Yes. Most clients launch a focused first version, learn from real users and add features in later phases. It is usually the most cost-effective approach.

Do you work with clients outside India?+

Yes. We work with clients across India, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait, the UK, Europe and the US, with quotes in your local currency.
