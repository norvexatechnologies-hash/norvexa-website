Overview

Most AI projects stall because they start with the technology instead of the work. The Norvexa AI audit starts with your teams and processes, so the first project you fund is the one most likely to pay back.

In two to four weeks we map how work really happens, score every opportunity on value and feasibility, and hand over a clear report and roadmap your leadership can act on.

FAQ

AI Readiness Audit: common questions

How long does the AI audit take?+

Typically two to four weeks, depending on the number of departments in scope.

What do we receive at the end?+

An audit report, a scored list of opportunities and a phased AI roadmap with costs and metrics.

Are we obliged to build with Norvexa afterwards?+

No. The roadmap is yours to execute with any partner or in-house team.

Who needs to be involved from our side?+

An executive sponsor plus a few hours from each department lead and a technical contact.
