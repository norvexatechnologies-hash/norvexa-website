The challenge

Sohar Services wanted to win local AC cleaning and maintenance jobs from Google search, with customers able to call or WhatsApp immediately.

What we built

  • Local-SEO landing page targeting AC cleaning and maintenance in Sohar
  • Call and WhatsApp buttons in the header, the hero and a floating button on every page
  • Why-choose-us section and service cards for split AC, window AC and maintenance
  • Local FAQ on servicing frequency in Sohar’s heat and dust
  • Lightweight, fast-loading, mobile-first build
